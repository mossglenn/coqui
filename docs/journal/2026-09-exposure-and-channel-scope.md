# 2026-09-01 — Exposure, and why the objection channel is not a second factor

**Supersedes** `design/content-accuracy-validation-plan.md` Phase 4's *alternate within-subject,
six items each, order counterbalanced*. **Current truth:** `design/review-motion-fork.md`
§Exposure is between-subject, and §Why the objection channel is not crossed with this fork.

---

## Two clean items, reached by division

Phase 2 sourced four clean MultipleChoice items specifically because two *is not a measurement* —
the fork is exercised only on clean MC, and six is where a bulk-confirm rate is a rate rather than
an anecdote. The fork's sequencing bullet then said *Phase 6 runs A, B and D*, and Phase 4 said
variants alternate within-subject at six items each. **Three variants across twelve items gives
each variant four, of which two are clean MultipleChoice** — the rejected number, arrived at by
dividing the sourcing rather than spending it.

Neither document was wrong when it was written. The division only exists once the fork has three
variants instead of two, and nothing re-read the arithmetic when D was added.

Resolved as **between-subject**: one variant per reviewer, full session each, six clean MC per
variant. The cost is recorded in the specification rather than left to be discovered at Phase 7 —
*n* = 1 per cell, variant fully confounded with reviewer, and what survives is within-person and
per-item rather than any rate compared across variants.

**It makes a recruiting question harder, and that is the honest price.** Phase 7's *one reviewer or
two* becomes *three, or the fork does not resolve on session evidence.*

## The objection channel as a crossed factor, considered and refused

The natural next thought is to cross the motions with placements of the objection channel — A1/A2,
B1/B2, D1/D2. Three arguments against, in order of how much they matter.

**B2 is not a second B. It is the repair for B's known defect.** The fork's objection to B is that
the stem claim loses its composition moment and comment has no home short of the flag control. An
ambient channel fixes exactly that. A-against-B asks whether survey beats sequential; B-against-B2
asks whether the fix works. The second question is real and it is downstream of the first.

**The factors interact by construction.** A and D already carry `[c] comment` on the stem row; B
carries nothing. So A1→A2 changes almost nothing while B1→B2 changes B's central objection. That is
an interaction, and an interaction is the one thing six cells at one reviewer each are worst at
detecting. Doubling the scarcest input to make the primary comparison less attributable is a bad
trade in both directions at once.

**Placement is a salience variable**, which is the confound §Why the escape hatch is not a fourth
variant already refused: a control on the primary row and one in a footer draw different rates from
the same reviewers holding the same beliefs. Crossing it with motion re-imports that confound one
level down. The fork rejected C for this and would have re-adopted it under another name.

## What replaced it

**One placement, applied identically to all three**, plus a prediction registered before the run:

> In B, comment on the stem claim will be under-represented relative to A and D, and objections
> will migrate into the flag control.

Directional and presence/absence rather than a rate comparison, which is what makes it readable at
one reviewer per cell, and consistent with Phase 7's own rule that direction can be pre-registered
where magnitude cannot. **Holding the channel constant does not cost the channel finding — it is
what produces one.** Adding B2 would have destroyed it, by removing the condition the prediction is
about.

**And expert attention is the wrong instrument for placement.** Motion needs domain experts because
the cost being measured is domain recall. Findability does not. That asymmetry is worth keeping in
view whenever a second factor is proposed for Phase 6: the question is not only *can the design
detect it*, but *does answering it require the scarce input at all.*
